The problem

Access is the hard part

A useful answer shouldn’t
cost someone their privacy.

Organizations need knowledge to be searchable, but internal files carry different permissions, owners, and levels of sensitivity.

01 / FRAGMENTED

Knowledge is scattered.

Policies, procedures, contracts, and team knowledge live across folders and documents.

02 / ACCESS DRIFT

Search can cross a boundary.

A shared index or generic assistant can expose material beyond the requester’s permissions if access isn’t enforced during retrieval.

03 / LOW TRUST

Answers need evidence.

Teams need to verify where an answer came from and administrators need a record of what happened.

The product opportunity: AI search that respects the same audience rules as the source documents.
