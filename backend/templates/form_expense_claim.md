# Expense Claim Form

This built-in template is deployable with the backend (including Vercel). It is
used when the company has not uploaded its own expense-claim template.

## Template

```text
EXPENSE CLAIM FORM - [Company]

1. EMPLOYEE DETAILS
* Full Name: [Full Name]
* Employee ID: [Employee ID]
* Department: [Department]
* Manager: [Manager's Name]

2. EXPENSE DETAILS
* Date: [Expense Date]
* Category: [Expense Category]
* Amount: [Amount]
* Currency: [Currency]
* Receipt attached: [Receipt attached]

3. APPROVAL
* Submitted by: [Full Name]
* Approved by: [Manager's Name]
* Approval date: To be completed by approver
```
