## 2. Policy area: Retention Schedule

- Every record category has a defined retention period.
- Legal holds suspend destruction for the affected records.
- Archived records are stored in immutable storage.
- Destruction is logged and witnessed by two authorised staff.
- File naming follows the group convention with a document code.
- All retention schedule records are reviewed by Group Secretariat at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of retention schedule.
- Questions about retention schedule are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to retention schedule requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for retention schedule are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
