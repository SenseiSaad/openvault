## 2. Policy area: Karachi

- The Karachi office is the group headquarters and primary engineering hub.
- The Lahore office hosts the customer success and finance teams.
- The Islamabad office supports public-sector engagements.
- Dubai serves as the regional hub for Middle East operations.
- London coordinates European customers and regulatory engagement.
- All Karachi records are reviewed by Group Facilities at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of Karachi.
- Questions about Karachi are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to Karachi requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for Karachi are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
