## 2. Policy area: Register

- The group risk register is reviewed by the board each quarter.
- Risks are scored on likelihood and impact.
- Key controls are mapped to ISO 27001 and SOC 2 criteria.
- Internal Audit publishes a rolling 12-month plan.
- External audits are coordinated by Group Compliance.
- All register records are reviewed by Group Compliance at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of register.
- Questions about register are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to register requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for register are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
