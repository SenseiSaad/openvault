## 2. Policy area: Integrity

- Employees act with integrity in all business dealings.
- Conflicts of interest must be declared to the compliance team.
- Gifts above USD 50 must be logged in the gifts register.
- Confidential information must not be discussed in public areas.
- Business attire is expected for client-facing meetings.
- All integrity records are reviewed by People & Culture at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of integrity.
- Questions about integrity are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to integrity requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for integrity are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
