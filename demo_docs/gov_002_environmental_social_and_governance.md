## 2. Policy area: Emissions

- Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions are reported annually.
- Office electricity consumption is tracked per square metre.
- Recycling rates are published in the ESG dashboard.
- Suppliers are asked to confirm their own environmental standards.
- Diversity metrics are included in the annual people report.
- All emissions records are reviewed by Group Sustainability at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of emissions.
- Questions about emissions are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to emissions requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for emissions are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
