## 2. Policy area: Annual Leave

- Employees accrue 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Casual leave is capped at 10 working days and cannot be combined with sick leave.
- Sick leave beyond three consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
- Parental leave is granted in line with the law of the employing country.
- Public holidays follow the official calendar published for each office location.
- All annual leave records are reviewed by People & Culture at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of annual leave.
- Questions about annual leave are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to annual leave requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for annual leave are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
