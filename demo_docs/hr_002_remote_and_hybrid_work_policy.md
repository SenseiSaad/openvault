## 2. Policy area: Hybrid Schedule

- Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time.
- A one-time home office allowance of USD 400 is available on approval.
- Monthly internet reimbursement is capped at USD 35 per employee.
- Employees must maintain a safe, ergonomic workspace at home.
- All hybrid schedule records are reviewed by People & Culture at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of hybrid schedule.
- Questions about hybrid schedule are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to hybrid schedule requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for hybrid schedule are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
