## 2. Policy area: Fire Safety

- Evacuation maps are displayed on every floor.
- Fire drills are conducted twice per year in each office.
- Certified first aiders are available on each shift.
- Ergonomic assessments are offered to all desk-based staff.
- All safety incidents are logged within 24 hours.
- All fire safety records are reviewed by Group Facilities at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of fire safety.
- Questions about fire safety are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to fire safety requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for fire safety are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
