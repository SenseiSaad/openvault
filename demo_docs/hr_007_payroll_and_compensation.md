## 2. Policy area: Pay Cycle

- Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month.
- Overtime requires prior manager approval and is paid at 1.5x the base rate.
- Statutory deductions are applied according to local law.
- Annual bonuses are paid in March following the performance cycle.
- Employees are paid in the currency of their employing entity.
- All pay cycle records are reviewed by Group Finance at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of pay cycle.
- Questions about pay cycle are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to pay cycle requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for pay cycle are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
