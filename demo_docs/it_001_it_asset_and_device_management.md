## 2. Policy area: Laptop

- All corporate laptops are enrolled in mobile device management.
- The asset inventory is reconciled quarterly.
- Devices are assigned to a named employee at issue.
- Operating system patches are applied within 14 days of release.
- Standard laptop refresh cycle is 42 months.
- All laptop records are reviewed by Group IT Operations at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of laptop.
- Questions about laptop are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to laptop requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for laptop are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
