## 2. Policy area: Joiner

- Access is provisioned through the joiner-mover-leaver workflow.
- Single sign-on is mandatory for all internal applications.
- Role-based access groups are maintained by application owners.
- Privileged access requires approval and session recording.
- Access reviews run every quarter for all systems.
- All joiner records are reviewed by Group IT Operations at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of joiner.
- Questions about joiner are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to joiner requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for joiner are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
