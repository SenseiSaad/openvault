## 2. Policy area: Severity

- Incidents are classified SEV-1 to SEV-4 by business impact.
- SEV-1 incidents trigger the on-call bridge within 15 minutes.
- Containment actions are prioritised over root-cause analysis.
- Affected systems are rebuilt from known-good images where possible.
- Regulatory notifications are assessed within 24 hours.
- All severity records are reviewed by Group Information Security at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of severity.
- Questions about severity are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to severity requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for severity are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes
