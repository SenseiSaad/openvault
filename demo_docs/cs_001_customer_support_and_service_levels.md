## 2. Policy area: Ticket

- Support tickets are categorised P1 to P4 by customer impact.
- P1 first response target is 30 minutes, 24x7.
- P2 first response target is two business hours.
- Resolution targets are published in the customer agreement.
- Escalation to engineering uses the on-call rota.
- All ticket records are reviewed by Group Customer Success at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of ticket.
- Questions about ticket are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to ticket requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for ticket are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
