## 2. Policy area: Booking

- Travel must be booked through the approved travel platform.
- Economy class applies for flights under six hours.
- Hotel caps are USD 180 per night in tier-1 cities.
- Per diem is paid at the published country rate.
- Receipts are required for every expense above USD 25.
- All booking records are reviewed by Group Finance at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of booking.
- Questions about booking are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to booking requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for booking are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
