## 2. Policy area: Zero Tolerance

- Harassment of any kind is not tolerated on company premises or platforms.
- Reports may be filed with HR, Legal or the anonymous hotline.
- Investigations start within five working days of a report.
- Support is offered to all parties throughout the process.
- Bystanders are encouraged to intervene safely or report.
- All zero tolerance records are reviewed by People & Culture at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of zero tolerance.
- Questions about zero tolerance are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to zero tolerance requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for zero tolerance are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.
