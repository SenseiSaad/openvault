## 2. Policy area: Lms

- Mandatory compliance courses are assigned on joining.
- The learning management system records completion evidence.
- Professional certifications may be reimbursed up to USD 1,500 per year.
- Tuition support requires a two-year service agreement.
- Conference attendance needs manager and budget approval.
- All LMS records are reviewed by Learning & Development at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of LMS.
- Questions about LMS are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to LMS requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for LMS are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
