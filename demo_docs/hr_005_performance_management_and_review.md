## 2. Policy area: Objectives

- Objectives are set at the start of each performance year.
- A formal mid-year check-in is held every July.
- Annual reviews close by 31 January of the following year.
- The rating scale has five levels from Needs Improvement to Outstanding.
- Calibration sessions align ratings across departments.
- All objectives records are reviewed by People & Culture at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of objectives.
- Questions about objectives are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to objectives requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for objectives are published on the intranet.
