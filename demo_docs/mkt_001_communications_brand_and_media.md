## 2. Policy area: Brand

- Brand guidelines define logo usage, colour and typography.
- External communications use a clear, plain-language tone.
- Press enquiries are routed to Group Communications.
- Social media posts require approval from the brand team.
- Standard document templates are published on the intranet.
- All brand records are reviewed by Group Communications at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of brand.
- Questions about brand are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to brand requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for brand are published on the intranet.
