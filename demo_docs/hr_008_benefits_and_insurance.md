## 2. Policy area: Health Insurance

- All permanent employees are enrolled in group health insurance.
- Dependants may be added during the annual enrolment window.
- Life cover equals two times annual base salary.
- Dental and vision benefits are included in the core plan.
- Statutory social security contributions are made for eligible staff.
- All health insurance records are reviewed by People & Culture at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of health insurance.
- Questions about health insurance are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to health insurance requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for health insurance are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
