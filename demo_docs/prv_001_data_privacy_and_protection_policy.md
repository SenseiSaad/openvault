## 2. Policy area: Personal Data

- Personal data is processed only for a documented lawful purpose.
- Consent must be freely given, specific and withdrawable.
- Retention schedules are defined per data category in the records register.
- Data subjects may request access to their data within 30 days.
- Cross-border transfers require an approved transfer mechanism.
- All personal data records are reviewed by Group Data Protection Office at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of personal data.
- Questions about personal data are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to personal data requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for personal data are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
