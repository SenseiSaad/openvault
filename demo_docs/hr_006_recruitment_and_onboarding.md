## 2. Policy area: Requisition

- Hiring starts with an approved requisition and budget check.
- Candidate sourcing uses approved channels and agencies.
- Structured interviews use a common scorecard across panels.
- Offers are issued only after background checks clear.
- Probation lasts three months for all new hires.
- All requisition records are reviewed by Talent Acquisition at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of requisition.
- Questions about requisition are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to requisition requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for requisition are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
