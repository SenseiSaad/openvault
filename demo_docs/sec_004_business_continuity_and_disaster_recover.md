## 2. Policy area: Bia

- Business impact analysis is refreshed annually.
- Maximum RTO for customer-facing platforms is four hours.
- Maximum RPO for transactional data is 15 minutes.
- Backups are stored in a separate geographic region.
- Failover tests run quarterly.
- All BIA records are reviewed by Group Resilience at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of BIA.
- Questions about BIA are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to BIA requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for BIA are published on the intranet.
