## 2. Policy area: Access Control

- All company systems require multi-factor authentication for interactive login.
- Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 or an approved equivalent.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters or use a managed passphrase.
- Information is classified Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
- Suspected security incidents must be reported within 60 minutes.
- All access control records are reviewed by Group Information Security at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of access control.
- Questions about access control are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to access control requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for access control are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
