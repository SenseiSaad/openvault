## 2. Policy area: Company Devices

- Company laptops may be used for reasonable personal activity outside work hours.
- Software must be installed from the approved catalogue only.
- Confidential data must never be stored in personal cloud accounts.
- Removable media is blocked by default on managed endpoints.
- The company monitors endpoint telemetry for security purposes only.
- All company devices records are reviewed by Group Information Security at each quarterly cycle.
- Regional leads in Karachi confirm local applicability of company devices.
- Questions about company devices are logged in the policy query register.
- Any change to company devices requires an impact note and an owner sign-off.
- Supporting templates for company devices are published on the intranet.

### 2.1 Operational notes

Managers in Islamabad are responsible for applying this section consistently. Exceptions must be recorded in the policy exception register with a named approver and an expiry date.

Evidence of compliance is retained for seven years in the group records repository. Internal Audit samples this section each quarter.
